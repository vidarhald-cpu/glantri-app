# Professions

These 84 Profession families describe occupational identities and the
training opportunities they can support. They are not character classes and
grant no competency, office, equipment, status, or resources.

The availability envelopes below are supported reference content, not active
Setting filters. Every one of the 203 individual opportunities remains usable
in the general/unbound reference but unresolved as a certified contextual
acquisition route.

See the [competency rules](../competency-rules.md) and [availability gaps
and exceptions](./gaps-and-exceptions.md).

## Hunter and Trapper

**Game-world description.** A hunter or trapper finds game and obtains it through a practised method suited to the quarry.

**Learning Groups.** [Tracking and hunting practice](learning-groups.md#group-g03) — Tracking-hunt route: Following and approaching game teaches tracking, hunting, concealment of movement and deliberate observation together.; [Trapline and trail practice](learning-groups.md#group-f34) — Trapline route; alternative or additional apprenticeship: Repeatedly laying and revisiting traplines teaches both trail reading and animal trapping.; [Field travel and camp practice](learning-groups.md#group-g04) — Expedition route: Extended supervised journeys teach camp provision, route choice and weather judgment.; [Longbow hunting apprenticeship](learning-groups.md#group-f41) — Longbow-hunting tradition only: The teacher coaches complete hunting and bow practice, including the narrower Longbow practice.

**Direct opportunities.** [Bow](skills.md#skill-bow) — Non-longbow bow-hunting route: Repeated coached shooting develops Bow; [Military bow and formation practice](learning-groups.md#group-f38) adds military formation and [Longbow hunting apprenticeship](learning-groups.md#group-f41) adds Longbow, neither of which an ordinary bow-hunting apprenticeship necessarily teaches.

**Notable competency areas.** [Tracking](skills.md#skill-skill-tracking); [Hunting](skills.md#skill-skill-hunting); [Stealth](skills.md#skill-skill-stealth); [Perception](skills.md#skill-skill-perception); [Trapping](skills.md#skill-skill-trapping); [Survival](skills.md#skill-skill-survival); [Wayfinding](skills.md#skill-skill-wayfinding); [Weather Lore](skills.md#skill-skill-weather-lore); [Bow](skills.md#skill-bow); [Longbow](skills.md#specialization-longbow)

**Explicit tracks.**

- None. Named training routes above are opportunities, not separate Profession tracks.

**Supported Society Level.** 1–6. This is the family's ordinary structural envelope; no opportunity inherits it as a certified floor.

**Supported Social Bands.** Available: Common Folk, Trades and Guilds, Established Households; Unresolved: Court and Elite.

**Supported Arch-Society applicability.** Available: Forager / ritual-tribal, Pastoral clan / nomadic warrior, Early agrarian chiefdom / proto-state, Bronze Age palace state, Temple-state / early literate agrarian state, Imperial agrarian bureaucracy, Feudal / manorial kingdom, Classical polis / city-state civilization, Imperial classical / Hellenistic-Roman high civilization, Court-bureaucratic high medieval empire, Commercial renaissance city-state / principality.

**Unresolved context and bindings.** Environmental/geographic context where it changes learned route practice. Desert context is normally a binding, not a Specialization. Environment, seasonal pattern and knowledge tradition; universal local competence is not implied. Quarry, environment and hunting practice; weapon competencies remain separate. Environmental/geographic context where the learned practice differs. Quarry, environment and animal-trap practice. Longbow weapon practice; Longbow remains a Specialization of Bow.

**Boundary.** Hunting does not supply a complete human close-combat curriculum, military scouting or every weapon used to take game.
