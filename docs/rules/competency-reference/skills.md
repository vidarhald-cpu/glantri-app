# Skills

This is the complete game-facing reference for the 131 approved base Skills.
Every cross-link is derived from the approved 59 Learning Groups and 84
Profession families. The final section records all 26 approved
Specializations so that parentage and explicit routes remain visible.

Availability is general and unbound unless an entry states a required
context. No entry below claims a completed Society-Level, Social-Band,
Arch-Society, or Setting-specific route.

See the [competency rules](../competency-rules.md) and [availability gaps
and exceptions](./gaps-and-exceptions.md).

## Base Skills

<a id="skill-skill-deception"></a>

### Deception

**Coverage.** Deception is the practice of making a misleading account or presentation convincing and keeping it consistent under scrutiny.

**Relevant Specializations.** None.

**Learning Groups.** [Impersonation and distraction practice](learning-groups.md#group-g16)

**Profession relations.** [Investigator and Intelligence Agent](professions.md#profession-p17) (through [Impersonation and distraction practice](learning-groups.md#group-g16)); [Confidence Trickster](professions.md#profession-p19) (through [Impersonation and distraction practice](learning-groups.md#group-g16))

**Supported availability.** General/unbound. No named contextual binding is currently required.

**Boundaries.**

- Disguise changes apparent identity; Deception sustains misleading claims.
- Acting creates a dramatic performance and does not automatically make a lie convincing.
- Insight interprets another person's reactions; Deception does not reveal their thoughts.

**Coverage.** Palaeography & Decipherment is the analysis of difficult, unfamiliar or historical writing to recover a defensible reading.

**Learning Groups.** [Historical text investigation](learning-groups.md#group-f15)
