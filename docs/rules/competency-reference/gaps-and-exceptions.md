# Competency availability gaps and exceptions

This report keeps unresolved content visible without weakening the approved
Skill → Learning Group → Profession structure. It is a reader and Product
Owner review companion, not an alternative catalogue.

| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Base Skills                | 131/131 have a name, concise and substantive description, boundaries, Learning Group reverse links, Profession reverse links, and an honest availability statement. | Contextual availability is not yet bound to particular Settings or societies.                                                       |

No approved base Skill, Learning Group, or Profession family is missing its
reader description. The one package-content gap is Scribe and Copyist's
unbound written core, described below.

## Availability gaps

### Contextual activation remains incomplete

The generic reference is usable. Context-sensitive play still needs the
following separate work:

- 84 family envelopes record supported Society-Level, Social-Band, and
  Arch-Society reference states, but do not activate filters.
- Every one of the 203 Profession training opportunities is marked usable as a
  generic reference opportunity and unresolved as a bound acquisition route.
- The dossier contains 140 unresolved context records: 27 explicit required
  bindings, 89 practice or subject descriptors, and 24 Profession-context
  annotations.
- No opportunity inherits a certified structural floor or admission rule from
  its Profession family.
- A Profession's presence does not prove the availability of every track,
  Learning Group, direct competency, teacher, institution, material, or piece
  of equipment associated with it.

Required bindings include actual languages and Written Traditions, law and
legal forums, religions, waters and navigation traditions, illicit milieus,
materials and technologies, military equipment and traditions, alchemical or
divinatory traditions, court systems, performance traditions, and games.

### Scribe and Copyist

Scribe and Copyist has a real occupational description and access to
Records-office practice, but the core writing route cannot identify an exact
Written Tradition until a Setting supplies one of the approved traditions or
authorizes later content.

This is the only approved Profession whose defining competency package has an
explicitly unbound core outside the 203 exact opportunities. It must not be
presented as universal literacy.

### Forager / ritual-tribal Common-Folk protection route

The Forager / ritual-tribal Arch Society still needs a credible Common-Folk
communal or civilian protection-and-fighting route. Guard, Watcher and Escort
through Guard and rough-fighting drill is the existing candidate, not an
approved final binding.

Completion requires evidence for:

- the full fighting curriculum;
- suitable equipment;
- structural support;
- a relevant martial and social tradition; and
- ordinary Common-Folk access.

Hunting alone is not sufficient. This gap blocks final contextual availability
for that route, not the generic Guard or Soldier Professions.

### Competencies without a generic Profession route

Two approved competencies deliberately have no generic Profession
association:

- **Poisons**: no poison-practice lineage has been approved, and attaching it
  to every Herbalist or Alchemist would overstate their training.
- **Warded-Lock Entry**: no warded-lock specialist occupation has been
  approved, and ordinary Thief or Lockpicking routes do not establish it.

They remain valid competencies and may receive separately authored individual
or contextual routes later.

### Possible shared-practice groups not added

Two reusable practices remain possible future Learning Group questions:

| Possible practice                      | Possible members                 | Current treatment                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| General bow-hunting apprenticeship     | Bow; Hunting                     | Hunter and Trapper uses direct Bow alongside an actually taught hunting practice. Military bow practice adds formation; Longbow hunting adds the Longbow Specialization, so neither is a generic substitute. |
| General animal-training apprenticeship | Animal Handling; Animal Training | Animal Trainer and Falconer uses direct routes. Falconry apprenticeship also teaches Hunting and Falconry; the other animal groups teach materially different practices.                                     |

Neither gap authorizes a sixtieth group.

### Language and writing

The approved target contains 12 spoken-language competencies and three Written
Traditions. None has a generic Learning Group membership or exact generic
Profession association. Scribe and Copyist provides only a type-appropriate,
unbound writing route.

Nine recovered language or binding cases remain unresolved:

| Case             | Exact unresolved question                                                                                               |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Common           | Define the written tradition's script, register, and conventions without granting it from Spoken Common.                |

| Imperial Aramaic | Separate language, script, and administrative written tradition using explicit evidence.                                |
| Linear B         | Specify the language, tradition, and script support and confirm the proposed pairing.                                   |
| Old Common       | Define its written-tradition identity and support script without inferring equivalence with Common.                     |

| Royal N'kolian   | Resolve language, register or tradition, and script support.                                                            |
| Runic Scandian   | Specify the Scandian written tradition and support script rather than infer them from the compound name.                |

### Learning Group and Profession exceptions

- Explicit Specialization membership supplies access but does not waive
  immediate-parent eligibility.
- Profession opportunities are additive family menus unless an explicit track
  says otherwise. A route label is not automatically a track.
- Sailor and Boat Worker treats Rigged-vessel deck apprenticeship and Working a
  boat as alternative purchases through that Profession.
- Guard, Watcher and Escort treats Swashbuckling fencing instruction and
  One-handed edged close-combat practice as alternative purchases. One-handed
  edged practice may still combine with Guard and rough-fighting drill when
  both are taught.
- Generic/unbound Character generation does not inherit legacy Society-Level
  or Social-Band filters.

training, availability, Social Band, authority, or competency association.

| Bounty Hunter       | Historical/legacy-only       | Preserve the apprehension-specialist identity; do not invent investigative duties or legal authority.                                                   |

| Crafter             | Unresolved                   | Recover the actual craft. Do not create a generic Crafter family.                                                                                       |

stands at 69 mapped or reconciled, 22 historical/legacy-only, and 5 unresolved.

## Source conflicts and relationship audit

### Resolved source composition

The original full Learning Group source contains 52 groups. The approved
martial revision replaces four pages and adds seven groups, producing the
controlling 59-group catalogue. It adds 36 base-Skill memberships and one
Specialization membership without removing an earlier target membership.

The final reverse links therefore use the 59-group catalogue, not the older
annotations embedded in the Skill prose source. In particular:

- One-handed edged close-combat practice, Swashbuckling fencing instruction,
  Spear/sidearm/throwing formation drill, and Mounted lance and troop drill use
  their revised memberships.
- Guard and rough-fighting drill, One-handed arms and formation drill,
  Two-handed military weapon practice, the three military ranged practices,
  and Longbow hunting apprenticeship are added.
- 1-h conc./axe, 2-h conc./axe, 2-h edged, Bow, Brawling, Crossbow, Firearms,
  Throwing, and Longbow must not be reported as groupless.

This is a versioned source composition, not an unresolved catalogue conflict.

### Open provenance count conflict

The legacy Profession source claims 518 associations. Its printed ledger
contains 523 occurrences: 49 family-scoped and 474 Profession-scoped, including
the same five-entry healer sequence in both scopes. No evidence identifies
which five rows, if any, are erroneous or differently owned.

All 523 printed occurrences remain preserved. Exact certification of 518 stays
blocked until an authoritative correction or the underlying scoped healer
ownership evidence is supplied. This discrepancy does not alter the 84 target
families or their 203 target opportunities.

### Relationship consistency

The approved references resolve all target relationship identities:

- every one of the 160 Learning Group memberships names an approved
  competency with the correct base-Skill or Specialization type;
- every Specialization membership preserves its immediate parent;
- every one of the 115 Profession group opportunities names one of the 59
  approved groups;
- every one of the 88 direct Profession opportunities names an approved Skill
  or Specialization;
- all 59 Learning Groups have at least one occupational route;
- Profession and Learning Group reverse links are derived from those same
  forward relationships rather than inferred from prose.

Historical associations, Skill-source annotations, semantic similarity, and
shared names are not used to add relationships.

### Profession description/package audit

No contradiction was found between the 84 approved Profession descriptions and
their explicit packages. All named route targets resolve, and the 21 explicit
military tracks contain the equipment and complete close-combat combinations
claimed by their descriptions.

One intentional gap remains: Scribe and Copyist describes writing and copying
but cannot name its defining Written Tradition in the generic package. That
core stays explicitly unbound rather than being replaced with generic
Literacy. The Poisons and Warded-Lock Entry coverage gaps are absence of a
generic occupational route, not contradictions in another Profession's
package.

### Learning Group name/member audit

No mismatch was found between an approved Learning Group name and its member
list. The seven complete close-combat groups contain Dodge, Parry, and an
actual weapon family. The military ranged groups explicitly contain the named
ranged Skill plus Formation Fighting. Fishing-boat, rigged-vessel,
longbow-hunting, and specialist workshop names match their exact memberships.

Working a boat and Metalworking apprenticeship remain intentional one-Skill
groups. Their sustained practices are narrower than nearby composite groups;
their size is not a name/member defect.
