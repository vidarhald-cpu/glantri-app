## Group notes

- **K14 Bedside attendance practice.** Answers the previously zero-Skill attendant package without borrowing the physician's curriculum.
- **K15 Birth-attendance practice.** Implements the approved Medicine -> Obstetrics correction (#231): the immediate parent is now supplied by the same practice.
- **F04 Running and athletic field practice.** Revised: Swimming and Climbing added so conditioned movement is taught as one practice.
- **N01 Small-craft boat working.** Replaces the one-Skill group G06 'Working a boat', whose taught practice was never only Seamanship.
- **F09 Rigged-vessel deck apprenticeship.** Revised: aloft work and weather judgement are part of the same taught deck practice.
- **M01 Close-combat weapon practice.** Restores the historical Basic Melee Training choose-one slot and, with it, general 2-h edged access. The approved catalogue reached 2-h edged only through F37 on two Soldier tracks.
- **M02 Advanced close-combat weapon practice.** Restores the historical Advanced Melee Training choose-three slot. Product Owner ruling: pick 3 is accepted. The recovered import's choose-2 reading is retained as provenance, not as an open question.
- **F30 Swashbuckling fencing instruction.** Kept without a slot: the tradition is defined by its weapon, so an alternative family would not be the same practice.
- **M03 Formation arms drill.** Merges the three fixed formation groups. A soldier is charged for the arms actually issued and drilled, not for every weapon family the army fields.
- **F32 Mounted lance and troop drill.** Revised: Riding made explicit, and the sidearm becomes a slot instead of a fixed 1-h edged charge. The group-size review also removed Brawling: mounted troop drill teaches fighting from the saddle, not unarmed scuffling, and a fixed burden of seven needed a reason it did not have.
- **M04 Military missile and formation practice.** Restores the historical Basic Missile Training choose-one slot and merges the three one-weapon military ranged groups.
- **M05 Advanced missile practice.** Restores the historical Advanced Missile Training choose-three slot.
- **C01 General artisan apprenticeship.** Restores the historical Craft Specialty choose-one slot. The fixed members are the workshop conduct every apprenticeship teaches alongside the craft, not a second craft.
- **C02 Master artisan practice.** Restores the Advanced Craft Specialty slot. Its count is NOT settled: the executable data says choose one, its own prose says two specialties, and the master-artisan reading suggests three.
