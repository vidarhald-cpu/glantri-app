# Direct Profession Skill grants: what was withdrawn and what was kept

The first draft of this proposal was authored under breadth pressure and pushed every ordinary path
to at least ten. The Product Owner inverted that pressure: author coherent occupational training
first, then read breadth as a diagnostic. This is the honest accounting of the result.

This revision applies a second ruling on top of it: **direct Profession Skills are exceptional**
**occupational training routes, not a mechanism for reaching the breadth signal**, and high direct-Skill
counts are to be reviewed — a coherent cluster belongs in a Learning Group. Four such clusters were found
and moved: the Guard's watch duty into **K24**, the Thief's street theft into **K25**, the Diviner's and
Religious Practitioner's contemplative discipline into **K22**, and the Weapon Instructor's salle into
**M10**.

The 2026-09-21 Learning-Group membership ruling then moved two grants the other way, and only two. It
retires C03 Construction site practice and directs that **Surveying** be supplied through an existing
group or a **direct Profession route** where setting-out is genuinely learned through the occupation.
The site carpenter (**P39**) and the millwright (**P48**) are the two former C03 users with no other
route to Surveying and a genuine claim on it, so each gains it as a direct route. The shipbuilder and
the building designer gain nothing: the yard's lofting is not the ground measurement Surveying
describes, and the building designer already reaches it through F28 and F01. **Athletics is not
restored** to the Weapon Instructor, because the same ruling removed it from M10 as well.

Highest remaining direct-Skill count: 3. 4 Profession(s) still carry three or more — P16 Local Informant (3), P48 Millwright and Mechanician (3), P52 Trapmaker (3), P53 Cook (3) — and each is argued in the table below rather than trimmed to a number.

Direct Skill grants: **283 before, 106 after** — 184 withdrawn.

## Policy applied

- R1 — no generic instruction grant. Teaching is withdrawn wherever instructing others is not part of the occupation's own definition. Master artisans still reach it through C02, and Teachers through K07.
- R2 — no generic commerce top-up. Trade, Appraisal and Bargaining are withdrawn wherever a Learning Group already supplies the family's commercial practice, or where selling one's output is not itself a taught practice.
- R3 — no generic social polish. Customs is withdrawn wherever the curriculum has no social, ceremonial, courtly or cross-cultural element beyond ordinary life.
- R4 — no generic learned top-up. History, Philosophy, Politics, Religion, Geography, Mathematics, Mnemonics and Insight are withdrawn wherever a learned group already supplies the grounding and the Skill is not the family's own subject matter.
- R5 — no workshop garnish. Carpentry, Ropework, Mechanics and similar adjacent trades are withdrawn where they were decoration rather than taught craft, and kept where the material genuinely is the craft.
- R7 — a coherent cluster belongs in a Learning Group. Where a family's direct grants described a practice that is genuinely taught and practised together, they were moved into a group rather than left as exceptional routes: the Guard's watch duty into K24, the Thief's street theft into K25, the Diviner's and Religious Practitioner's contemplative discipline into K22, and the Weapon Instructor's salle into M10. Direct grants are exceptional occupational routes, not a breadth mechanism.
- R6 — a grant survives on coherence, never on arithmetic. Several retained grants leave their path at 7 or 8; that is an accepted outcome, not a defect to be corrected.

## Effect on the distribution

Median effective breadth moved from 3 in the approved catalogue, to 10 in the first draft, to 8 here. Quartiles are now 7 and 9. That is the intended shape: most paths sit in the 7-10 range on their own content, and the handful that run wider do so because the occupation genuinely is wide.
