## Question

What accepted qualitative architecture governs the next Glantri Skill Catalogue: its competence-bearing records, non-mechanical navigation, civilization-relative applicability, explicit relationships, training histories, language and specialization identities, and frozen player-facing domain structure?

## Conflict or limitation

Legacy workbooks and current runtime structures mix learnable competence, category membership, economic class, availability, groups, and relationships. They remain evidence, not automatic authority for the new catalogue. The accepted architecture separates those concerns.

The v0.1 synthesis deliberately carried provisional placement and family markers. The later audit rulings and v0.2 freeze resolve them. No contradiction remains among the accepted rulings consolidated here; where earlier provisional material differs, the later accepted ruling controls.

The integrated #365 contracts can represent and validate this architecture, but they are additive authoring infrastructure. They do not populate a final catalogue, enforce eligibility, price competence, alter Character state, or activate runtime behavior.

Quantitative mechanics and live activation remain deliberately unresolved. They must not be inferred from examples, the authoring schema, legacy behavior, or the proposals on #368.

## Ruling

### Authority and competence boundary

1. Domains and categories are stable navigation, presentation, discovery, and authoring structures. They are not purchasable or XP-bearing and create no automatic competence, grant, availability, prerequisite, cost, relationship, or transfer.
2. Concrete Skills and Specializations are the learnable, competence-bearing records. Stable identity and a clear task envelope—not a taxonomy label—determine whether a competence is distinct.
3. A Profession is not a Skill. A later Profession may assemble Skills, Specializations, access routes, and training histories, but role, title, or seniority does not replace the underlying competencies.
4. Every concrete competence has one primary navigation home. Optional secondary placements and contextual cross-references improve discovery only; they neither duplicate identity nor create mechanics.
5. Category, domain, family, shared name, shared product, or shared material membership never creates a mechanical relationship. Every mechanical relationship is separately authored, typed, and directional. A reverse relationship is never inferred.
6. The legacy `primary`/`secondary` classification is retired as future Skill semantics. It cannot imply competence, importance, inclusion, transfer, availability, or fictional scope; later economics must use a separately approved model.

### Civilization-relative applicability and access

1. DS1-DS6 is a generalized civilization/profile-relative knowledge-development frame. It describes the society's knowledge context, reproducible practices, tools, institutions, and likely differentiation; it is not Character competence or a universal Character progression ladder.
2. One concrete Skill may span several development stages when its method and task envelope remain materially the same. Stage transitions do not require replacement Skills. Names and descriptions must remain accurate across every authored profile/stage where the Skill applies.
3. Societies and profiles may vary by domain. The applicability model therefore preserves profile defaults, first-class domain-scoped DS/AB overrides, and concrete-competency overrides rather than forcing one universal catalogue ladder.
4. AB1-AB4 describes society-relative routes to learning opportunities—such as institutions, teachers, equipment, texts, and restricted traditions—not competence, XP, rank, or power. The bands are shared authoring/player orientation, not universal social-class names.
5. Biography, contact, institution, profile, teacher, text, and tradition are valid attributable bases for an applicability or access exception. Such metadata explains why an opportunity exists or differs; it grants no competence and does not itself calculate eligibility.
6. Exceptional access may depart from a profile or domain default where the authored biography or knowledge channel supports it. Detailed defaults, exception calibration, and runtime resolution remain deferred.

### Relationships, histories, languages, and Specializations

1. Relationships between concrete competencies are explicit authored directed edges. Accepted authoring kinds distinguish inclusion/containment, related transfer or familiarity, learning advantage, reinforcement, and prerequisite/base structure; the kind alone supplies no reverse edge or unapproved runtime mechanic.
2. This record assigns no relationship amount, prerequisite threshold, source combination, propagation, or runtime result. Each such behavior requires separate approval and authorship.
3. Learning/training groups represent narratable shared histories: for example a household, apprenticeship, workshop, school, crew, unit, office, campaign, institution, or comparable learning context. They are not semantic mega-groups or exclusive catalogue partitions.
4. Contextual groups may overlap and may share a Skill when each group explains a different plausible route by which it was learned. Mechanical synergy alone is not a valid learning context. Once learned, the competence has the same identity whether its provenance is a group or individual route.
5. Spoken language and written tradition are separate concrete competencies. A written tradition binds a language, a script, and the conventions for using them. A script or language-family identity supports authoring and discovery but never grants spoken or written competence automatically. There is no universal Literacy Skill.
6. A Specialization is a concrete learnable competence rooted in a genuine shared task or technical foundation with an authored base relationship/prerequisite structure. A shared product, category, material, or name is insufficient.
7. A valid Specialization may have zero transfer/related edges. Its base identity does not itself imply competence transfer, familiarity, a learning advantage, a reverse effect, or an amount. If any such relationship is later approved, each direction remains explicit.

### Locked cross-catalogue boundaries

- The fibre/textile structure is `Fibre Working` with `Cordage / Ropemaking`, separate-base `Weaving`, and `Tailoring` with `Sailmaking`. A textile product category does not justify a mega-parent.
- The siege structure is separate technical `Siege Engineering`, separate participant `Siege Engine Operation`, and `Siege Operations` under `Battle Tactics`. `Formation Command` remains a standalone organizational/command competence.
- `Teaching` has one identity: primary Social Interaction, secondary Scholarship.
- `Alchemy` has one identity: primary Craft & Making, secondary Scholarship and Natural Knowledge, with contextual Healing discovery where authored.
- `Trapmaking` is primary Covert Entry & Infiltration and is not animal Trapping or a perception/lock/mechanics substitute.
- Awareness & Investigation remains deliberately sparse and cross-reference-heavy. Catalogue symmetry and target domain size are usability considerations, not reasons to invent competence.

### Explicitly deferred quantitative and activation decisions

This record does **not** decide or activate:

- relationship percentages, strengths, source-selection arithmetic, or any numeric inclusion/transfer result;
- Skill economic weights, base costs, or the meaning of provisional scope/weight labels;
- learning-group pricing, contribution, overlap, or rebate economics;
- prerequisite thresholds;
- Specialization-to-base or base-to-Specialization effects;
- overlap or stacking policy, recursion/cascade/cycle policy, or rounding;
- detailed DS/AB defaults, applicability calibration, exception calibration, precedence arithmetic, or eligibility enforcement;
- migration or conversion arithmetic and Character preservation mechanics; or
- final catalogue population, canonical content activation, or any live runtime use.

The #368 recommendations remain proposals until separately accepted by the product owner/control plane. Even an accepted quantitative decision would still require a separately authorized implementation contract.

## Reasoning

The architecture preserves believable Character biography and civilization difference without turning a historical taxonomy into hidden rules. A concrete competence can travel across societies when its practice really is continuous, while profile/domain variation and attributable exceptions represent different knowledge environments without pretending that a stage or access label is Character ability.

Stable navigation helps players find a large catalogue, but separating discovery from mechanics prevents accidental transfer, pricing, and eligibility effects. Explicit directed relationships and genuine task-envelope tests keep similarity reviewable. Contextual overlapping learning histories describe how competence was learned without forcing every Skill into one semantic bundle.

The catalogue boundaries favor recurring player-facing tasks over encyclopedic symmetry. Sparse domains, distinct sibling practices, and a small craft family are valid when they tell players and GMs what a Character can actually attempt. Quantitative calibration is kept separate because cost, transfer, stacking, and migration choices require their own worked examples and balance review.

## Executable examples

These examples are qualitative authoring/validation assertions. They produce no live Character behavior.

1. **Navigation membership is inert.** Given separate `Wayfinding`, `Pilotage`, and `Seamanship` records with Navigation as their primary home and no relationship edges, validation accepts them and creates no transfer, prerequisite, cost, availability, or reverse edge.
2. **A zero-transfer Specialization is valid.** Given `Tailoring`, a `Sailmaking` Specialization with a valid authored Tailoring base/prerequisite placeholder, and no transfer edges, validation accepts the Specialization. No Tailoring-to-Sailmaking or Sailmaking-to-Tailoring competence is inferred.
3. **Cross-domain discovery preserves one identity.** Given one `Alchemy` record primary to Craft & Making and discoverable from Scholarship, Natural Knowledge, and an applicable Healing context, every discovery path resolves to the same record and creates no relationship or eligibility fact.
4. **Written competence must be explicit.** Given a spoken-language identity and a script identity, validation does not synthesize literacy. A distinct written-tradition record binding that language, script, and conventions is required before written competence can be authored.
5. **Applicability provenance grants no competence.** Given a profile/domain applicability record and an attributed teacher, contact, text, or tradition exception, validation preserves the stated basis and target. It does not award XP, rank, eligibility, or a Skill.
6. **Training histories may overlap.** Given a shipboard crew history and a merchant-voyage history that both include Seamanship for different narrated reasons, both groups are valid. Their overlap creates no exclusive ownership, inferred relation, or pricing result.
7. **The siege boundary remains explicit.** Given `Siege Engineering`, `Siege Engine Operation`, `Battle Tactics`, and a zero-transfer `Siege Operations` Specialization of Battle Tactics, validation preserves four distinct identities and their authored base structure without inventing any relationship arithmetic.
8. **Sparse discovery is valid.** Given Perception primary to Awareness & Investigation and inert cross-references to Insight and Tracking, validation requires no generic Investigation, Detect Lies, or Detect Traps Skill.

## Implementation consequences

- Authors and future catalogue work must preserve stable identities, user-facing descriptions, task envelopes, one primary domain, optional inert discovery metadata, explicit provenance/status, and explicit directed relationships.
- The additive [domain authoring contract](../../../packages/domain/src/content/skillCatalogueAuthoring.ts) and [content validator](../../../packages/content/src/skillCatalogueAuthoring/validator.ts) are the current representation/validation foundation. Their representative fixtures are proposal/test data, not the canonical catalogue.
- Authoring validation must continue to reject purchasable/XP-bearing domains or categories, missing/invalid relationship targets, implied reverse edges, malformed language/script/tradition identity, invalid specialization bases, and invalid DS/AB metadata, while keeping proposal/evidence/legacy status distinguishable from canonical records.
- Runtime Skills, Chargen, Character state, persistence, current imported content, and player UI remain unchanged. No migration, compatibility conversion, or live eligibility behavior follows from this record.
- Any quantitative rule, canonical catalogue population, live activation, or runtime consumer requires separately accepted product/rule decisions and a fresh authorized task contract.
