## D. Terminology and style guide

These conventions preserve the distinctions required by the authoring brief and the accepted localized-function approach. [S1; S5]

| Term or convention | Reusable guidance |
|---|---|
| **Dazed / stunned** | Describe disruption of thought or response, not unconsciousness. These words do not create extra conditions. Use a brief daze for the one-round head results and continuing difficulty gathering thoughts for the multi-round result; exact timing remains in the structured effects. |
| **Unconscious** | Use only for explicit unconsciousness. Prefer “loses consciousness” to “slumps”, “collapses” or “falls unconscious” when no fall operation exists. Unconsciousness does not supply a drop operation. |
| **Impaired** | The named function is difficult or unreliable, not wholly unavailable. Prefer concrete wording such as “awkward to use”, “unsteady” or “exertion is difficult”; do not broaden the affected function. |
| **Unusable** | State the loss of the specified hand, arm, leg or joint function plainly. It does not itself mean severing, permanent disability or a whole-body action lock. Standing/walking loss says nothing additional about unrelated actions. |
| **Bleeding** | Match external wounds with visible blood and internal wounds with blood collecting within the injured region. Do not add either type where absent, or infer death, shock progression or unconsciousness from bleeding. |
| **Lodged / transfixed / pinned** | “Lodged” means retained in the wound; “transfixed” describes penetration through the part. “Pinned” additionally asserts an attachment and needs specific support. None supplies a removal procedure, extra movement injury, or whole-body restraint by implication. |
| **Crushed / shattered / severed** | Distinguish compressive destruction, bone broken into fragments, and actual detachment. Keep “nearly severed” distinct from “severed”. Structural destruction does not automatically mean amputation. |
| **Burns** | Distinguish active flames, deep burns with systemic shock, and burns with airway injury. Retain only the particular accepted injury account; burn-fluid loss is not Bleed, and shock language is not an unconsciousness or mortality rule. |
| **Care and recovery** | Leave care generic unless a more specific classification is expressly accepted. Narrative should not create treatment checks, recovery times, permanence or later deterioration. |
| **Sentence construction** | Name the impact and injured part, then describe the immediate supported result. Prefer ordinary anatomy to medical jargon. Describe a fall or drop only where separately authorised; leave values and durations to the structured effects. |

## E. Exception / conflict list

### 1. `sharp|head|03` — unsupported blood-in-the-eyes imagery

The recovered narrative sends blood into the eyes, although the accepted result has neither Bleed nor a visual-impairment state. “Reeling” also risks suggesting unsupported loss of balance. The proposed narrative retains the scalp cut and Stun without these implications. [S2, remaining-29 table; S3, exact key]

### 2. `sharp|head|10`, `blunt|head|10`, `pointed_weapons|head|10` — unconsciousness coupled to positional wording

The recovered narratives use “slumps unconscious” or “collapses unconscious”, but these entries contain no `fall_to_ground` operation. The proposals say “loses consciousness” without adding a fall or a prone state. The change to integrated `sharp|head|10` remains an optional editorial proposal. [S2; S3, corresponding keys]

### 3. `falling_crushing|whole_body|10` — “normally” weakens the stated function loss

“Cannot stand or walk normally” leaves room for abnormal standing or walking, whereas the supplied functional result is standing/walking unusable. The proposal states “cannot stand or walk”, with no additional action enforcement. [S1; S2; S3, exact key]

### 4. `falling_crushing|whole_body|12` — current-prone field versus the narrative guardrail

REPORT.md and the reconstruction record **current prone**, but no `fall_to_ground` operation. Your narrative guardrail says not to imply prone when that operation is absent. The proposal therefore omits position altogether. This is not a decision to delete the recorded prone state, add a fall operation, or resolve the state/operation distinction through prose. [S1, Falling guardrail; S2, remaining-29 table; S3, exact key]

### 5. `pointed_weapons|abdomen|08` — movement/removal implies a new injury consequence

The recovered claim that movement or careless removal tears the wound wider prescribes additional injury beyond the accepted lodged-weapon, impairment, bleeding and care/removal content. The proposal describes the existing injury and difficulty acting instead; no new worsening rule is supplied. [S2; S3, exact key]

**No other unresolved conflict prevents these prose proposals.** The embedded-chest duration and the Fire/Energy rates are settled by the supplied rulings, not reopened questions. [S1]
