**Authority:** source/content authoring only, dated 15 September 2026. L is the controlling legacy artifact; P is the accepted target Profession proposal under the current user instruction. New reconciliation and availability judgments are labelled as authoring, not previously recovered decisions. No runtime migration or acquisition rule is implied.

## Binding boundary

Every bound value in this ledger is **null / Unresolved** because no setting-specific assignment was supplied. This preserves missing content rather than assigning universal languages, law, religion, waters, weapon traditions or an illicit milieu. Exact contexts are copied from accepted-scope documents and associated with the opportunities that invoke them.

There are two different kinds of work: an explicit required binding (such as Pilotage waters or a legal system) and a practice/subject description (such as a material or teaching tradition). The second does **not** become a new mandatory purchase, familiarity rule, gate or specialization. Family annotations may refer only to a specialist branch: a court-cookery context does not block ordinary meal preparation; an illicit-network binding does not make every guard, merchant or informant illicit.

An opportunity may require a working language without teaching it. Spoken and written competencies remain separate. The generic/unbound Profession catalogue may still be consumed in Chargen; these unresolved bindings limit claims about a particular contextual training opportunity.
