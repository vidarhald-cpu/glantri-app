## What is counted

The 21 military compositions from P section G are preserved separately below to show the combined/alternative tracks without inflating opportunity counts.

Admission is Conditional on that variant's actual service/apprenticeship and stated local restrictions. No branch inherits the family range as an automatic availability rule.
